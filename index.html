Also settle before you price

  • Does the price include or exclude 15% VAT?
  • What share of revenue does the university or college keep?
  • Will you charge IAU students and external students different prices?
  • Are faculty paid per item, per hour, or as part of their workload? Unpaid time is still a real cost.
  • Over how many years should the setup cost be recovered (the "spread" above)?
  • Content must be refreshed whenever the SCFHS SPLE blueprint or major guidelines change.
  • Refund policy, scholarships and early-bird or group discounts.
  • Conversion rate: how many students who see the offer actually pay (from your pilot willingness-to-pay data).
  • Pricing of competitors (SCFHS resources, FADIC, other universities) caps what you can charge.
  • Data protection (PDPL) and who owns the question bank.
